Privacy Policy for QR Master

Effective date: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how QR Master handles information when you use the QR Master Android application.

1. Information and Permissions

Camera: QR Master uses your device camera when you choose to scan QR codes. Camera access is used for scanning.

Local history: The app may store generated or scanned QR content in local app storage so you can view your history. This information is not sent to a QR Master server by the app's current implementation.

Gallery: If you choose to save a generated QR code, the image is saved to your device's gallery.

2. Sharing

If you choose to share a QR image or scanned result, the content is passed to the app or service you select. That service's own privacy policy may apply.

3. Accounts, Advertising and Analytics

The current version does not require you to create an account. This policy must be updated if advertising, analytics, or other third-party services are added.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Local history remains on your device until you delete it using available app features or remove the app's local data. Images saved to your gallery may need to be deleted separately.

5. Children's Privacy

QR Master is a general-purpose utility and is not specifically designed for children.

6. Security

Information stored locally may be accessible to someone with access to your device. Please keep your device secure.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. Updates will be published on this page.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact:

Email: support.qrmaster@gmail.com
